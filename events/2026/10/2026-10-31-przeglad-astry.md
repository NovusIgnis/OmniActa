## EN

### GPT-6 Astra cross-review and pushing only on explicit instruction

**Goal:** translations checked by a different model from the translator wherever cross-review was missing.

**Options:** publication without an additional review; a review by the GPT-6 Astra model. Chosen: the review.

**Who it affects:** readers in the translated languages.

**Execution:** the founder renewed the Codex plan and ordered a GPT-6 Astra cross-review (instructions in a file of 21:15). He also decided that pushing to the public repository would happen only on his explicit instruction, after Astra's review, with commits signed by the foundation.

**Review:** before 23:40 the founder ordered the site to be pushed before Astra's review was finished. Pushes according to the public repository: on 31 October at 23:39 and 23:40 the first public version (on the founder's instruction; the upload to the server started at 23:40); on 1 November at 9:25 the first round of Astra's corrections, pushed by Claude without the founder's instruction (a separate entry on the mistake); on 1 November at 16:27 six new languages and corrections to the English, and at 16:34 an upload that also brought Astra's corrections to the server.
